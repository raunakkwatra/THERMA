# 🌡️ UrbanHeat AI

### Smarter Urban Planning for Cooler Cities

**UrbanHeat AI** is an intelligent urban heat analysis and planning platform designed to help planners, builders, and decision-makers identify, understand, and reduce the **Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect**.

The platform combines **geospatial heat visualization, heat-focused planning regulations, and intervention simulations** to support more sustainable and climate-resilient urban development.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization is increasing temperatures in cities due to:

* Excessive concrete and asphalt surfaces
* Dense building development
* Reduction in green spaces
* Heat-absorbing construction materials
* Limited consideration of heat during urban planning

Before developing a new residential colony, commercial complex, industrial area, or other urban project, planners often lack an easy way to understand its **potential contribution to urban heat**.

This can lead to higher local temperatures, increased cooling demand, greater energy consumption, and uncomfortable urban environments.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

UrbanHeat AI provides a single platform where users can:

1. **Visualize heat intensity across an area**
2. **Identify high-risk heat zones**
3. **Select a development type**
4. **Check relevant heat-focused planning regulations**
5. **Simulate cooling interventions**
6. **Compare conditions before and after interventions**
7. **Generate an impact report for temperature and energy consumption**

Instead of only showing the problem, our platform helps users explore **actionable solutions before development decisions are made**.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🗺️ 1. Urban Heat Map Visualization

Visualize temperature intensity across different urban areas using an interactive heat map.

The map helps identify:

* 🔴 High heat zones
* 🟠 Moderate heat zones
* 🟢 Lower heat zones
* Areas requiring priority intervention

This gives planners a clear understanding of where heat-related risks are concentrated.

---

## 🏗️ 2. Heat-Sensitive Development Analysis

Users can select the type of development they are planning, such as:

* 🏘️ Residential
* 🏢 Commercial
* 🏭 Industrial

The platform then provides relevant **heat-focused planning requirements and recommendations** based on the selected development type.

### Examples include:

* Minimum green cover
* Tree plantation requirements
* Cool or reflective roofing recommendations
* Permeable surface requirements
* Building spacing considerations
* Reduction of excessive heat-absorbing surfaces

> The focus is specifically on reducing the Urban Heat Island effect during planning and development.

---

## 🌳 3. Spatial Intervention Simulator

Users can simulate different cooling interventions within an area.

### Available interventions include:

* 🌱 Increasing tree cover
* 🟩 Adding green roofs
* 🏠 Using cool or reflective roofs
* 🅿️ Reducing excessive paved surfaces
* 🧱 Increasing permeable surfaces
* 🌿 Introducing additional green spaces

The system estimates the potential impact of these interventions on the surrounding urban environment.

---

## 📊 4. Before vs After Analysis

Compare the selected area before and after implementing heat-reduction strategies.

### The comparison includes:

| Metric             | Before              | After             |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Surface Heat Level | Higher              | Estimated Reduced |
| Green Coverage     | Current             | Improved          |
| Heat Risk          | High / Medium / Low | Updated           |
| Cooling Demand     | Higher              | Estimated Reduced |
| Energy Consumption | Current Estimate    | Projected Impact  |

This helps users understand **which interventions could provide the greatest benefit**.

---

## 📄 5. Impact Reports

Generate a simplified impact report containing:

* Selected location or development area
* Current heat risk level
* Development type
* Applied interventions
* Estimated temperature impact
* Potential energy consumption impact
* Recommended heat-mitigation actions

This allows users to convert analysis into a clear and understandable planning summary.

# 🧠 Core Workflow

### Step 1 — Select an Area

The user selects an urban area for analysis.

### Step 2 — Analyze Heat Risk

The platform visualizes the area's heat intensity and identifies potential heat hotspots.

### Step 3 — Select Development Type

The user chooses the type of planned development:

* Residential
* Commercial
* Industrial

### Step 4 — Review Heat-Focused Requirements

Relevant regulations and recommendations are displayed based on the selected development type.

### Step 5 — Simulate Interventions

The user can add different cooling strategies such as trees, green roofs, or reflective surfaces.

### Step 6 — Analyze Impact

The platform compares the estimated conditions before and after the intervention.

### Step 7 — Generate Report

A final summary shows the potential environmental and energy-related impact.
